## 13-step process from idea to revenue

John says he has used this method in most of his products and that it "worked so well". The steps, in order:

1. Idea
2. SEO potential
3. Pick the least competitive option
4. Social validation
5. Waitlist
6. Organic promo
7. Paid promo
8. Directories and SEO
9. Check whether there is traction
10. Code the MVP
11. Turn the coding into marketing
12. Iterate
13. Public launch

- Validation, waitlist and promotion all come before the MVP is coded.
- Coding only starts at step 10, after the traction check in step 9.
- Step 11 treats the build itself as marketing material.
- The public launch comes last.
