## John Rush's tool stack: 78 paid products across 24 projects (May 2024)

- In May 2024 John said he pays for 78 products to run his 24 projects.
- He says it took "many trials and errors to find best tools on price/features/UX."
- He is open to switching: if someone knows a better alternative, he may move to it.
- He also uses 4 of his own products on top of the 78 (see the last section).

## Hosting, cloud and databases

- Hosting: Digital Ocean, AWS (hosting, storage, CDN), Hetzner (bare metal), Monovm, Google Cloud, Microsoft Azure (websites and tables), Vercel (Next.js hosting), appliku (web app deployment).
- Databases: Heroku for db hosting, Supabase as CMS and DB, MongoDB Atlas for NoSQL, Pinecone for vector db.
- Code: GitHub for teams.
- Cloudflare for DDoS protection, IPs, storage and AI.
- BunnyCDN for images.

## Dev, design and web tooling

- Design and docs: Figma for design and mockups, Canva for videos, Excalidraw for wireframes, Notion for docs, FloatUI as a Tailwind UI kit.
- Image handling: IMGIX for resizing, UploadCare for upload and storage.
- Scraping and automation: Apify for web automation, Scrapingbee to scrape the internet, ScreenshotOne as a screenshot API, Serper for Google search via API, Proxysell for proxies.
- Internal tooling: Fibery.
- Twilio for SMS.

## AI and LLMs

- OpenAI for LLMs and image generation, plus Azure OpenAI.
- Anthropic for the Claude LLM.
- ChatGPT as a separate subscription.
- Pinecone as the vector db.

## Testing, monitoring and security

- Server monitoring: New Relic.
- JS event logging: Sentry.
- Uptime monitoring: StatusCake and UptimeRobot (both).
- End-to-end testing: Ghost Inspector (visual), LambdaTest, Qase.
- Cloudflare for DDoS protection.

## Email, support and communication

- Email sending: SendGrid, Sensorpro, Sinch Mailjet, Mailtrap, AWS SES, Loops (campaigns).
- Google (G Suite) for email and drive.
- Support: Crisp and Intercom.
- Calls and internal comms: Zoom to record calls, Loom for internal async communication, TLDR to record and transcribe meetings.
- Enterprise clients: Slack for shared channels, Microsoft Teams, Skype for a shared US phone number.
- 1Password to share passwords across the team.
- Hubstaff to track hours to bill enterprise clients.

## Payments, marketing, legal and finance

- Payments: Stripe, Paddle, Gumroad.
- Affiliates: FirstPromoter for affiliate partners.
- Ads: Facebook ads and Google Ads.
- X (Twitter) for verified badges.
- Domains: GoDaddy and Namecheap.
- Ipostal for a postal address.
- App stores: Google Play for Android apps, Apple Developer account for iOS apps.
- Legal and finance: PandaDoc for legal paperwork and docs, QuickBooks for accounting, Mercury for banking, Carta for cap table.
- Other listed: Grammarly ("to fix my dyslexia"), VeeD for videos, Black Magic for Twitter, Zenvoice, Indie Hackers portal subscription, startups.com.
- A commenter suggested Lemon Squeezy for payments that handle taxes at a reasonable price; John replied, but the reply text isn't in the source.

## John's own products used in his stack

- Unicorn Platform: builds websites, waitlists, directories and landing pages.
- SeoBOT: researches and generates blog articles.
- ListingBott: grows domain rating and lists his products on all web directories with one click.
- IndexRusher: forces Google to index his pages faster.
