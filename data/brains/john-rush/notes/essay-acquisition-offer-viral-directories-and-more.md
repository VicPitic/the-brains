## Directories as a viral traffic play (osssoftware.org, allgpts.co, Unicorn Platform)

- John launched osssoftware.org and it took off. It reached an estimated 100k+ visits and made headlines on Hacker News, Twitter and dev.to. His takeaway: "you never know what will work until you try."
- allgpts.co hit 0.5M visits. It is a directory of GPTs.
- John says Unicorn Platform got a big push from its new "directory" template. Users can build directories quickly and at low cost.
- Unicorn Platform had its biggest single-month growth in paying users since launch, and paying-user growth has continued for 4 months in a row.
- He planned 2 new directories and further Unicorn directory features (such as nested pages) the following week.

## Virality is not revenue: Marketsy.ai after a #1 Product Hunt launch

- Marketsy.ai ranked #1 on Product Hunt and drew huge traffic, but few visitors converted into paying users.
- It is still searching for product-market fit.
- John launched a lifetime deal (LTD) on AppSumo to learn more about user patterns.
- His lesson: "Going viral doesn't always mean making money."

## Product Hunt versus tweets, Hacker News and dev.to

- Floatui v2, a free open-source Tailwind CSS UI kit, placed #6th on its Product Hunt launch. That fell short of his expectations.
- John is stepping back from promoting PH launches. He says they "don't seem valuable anymore" compared to tweets or posts on Hacker News or dev.to.
- Floatui is loved by developers and he plans to keep improving it.
- Nextjsstarter.com launched with around 1000 visitors. He calls that "not viral, but not bad either."

## Whether to sell or keep allgpts.co: acquisition offers and valuation checks

- John posted that he might sell allgpts.co and got 4 offers.
- To get a sense of pricing, he tried to list it on Acquire.com. The listing was initially blocked because the site had zero revenue. Acquire's founder (@agazdecki) fixed it quickly by DMing him.
- His takeaway: "Founders who handle customer support are bound to succeed."
- After seeing the stats, he decided to keep the site and build it into "Google for all kinds of gpts". It covers ChatGPT GPTs and also others such as POE and CharacterAI.

## Turning down VC interest in devhunt.org

- Devhunt.org got interest from top VCs, and John said no thanks.

- Indexgoogle.com was being tested internally across all his products before a launch the following week.
