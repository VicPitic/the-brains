## Pricing: cutting the cheapest plan to remove difficult users

- With seobotai v2, John removed the $19 plan.
- Reason: to cut out "difficult users" who drain his support agents.
- Principle: the lowest price tier can attract the customers who cost the most support, so dropping it protects support capacity.
