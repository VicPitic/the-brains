## Directory track record and exit (as of Sep 2024)

- John built his first directory, allGPTs.co, with no prior directory experience. He shared lessons publicly on Twitter over 12 months.
- allGPTs.co was acquired by ChippAI. He calls it his first "directory exit". The acquisition price was "5 figures" (no exact number given).
- He launched 20 directories in total. 5 turned out great, and he says they may become the best in their niche worldwide. Examples he names: nextjsstarter.com, osssoftware.org, mvpwizards.com.
- Across the directories he claims over 10 million users.
- He monetized them into passive income.
- He channeled directory traffic to his other SaaS tools, which he values at $1m+.

## Directories as a distribution and passive income asset

- John treats directories as a repeatable play: launch many, keep the 5 of 20 that work, and monetize or sell them.
- Monetization methods he lists: affiliate links, sponsors, paid listings.
- Directories also act as a traffic channel feeding his other SaaS products.
- Selling the directory is an explicit end state in his playbook, as the allGPTs.co exit shows.
