## The V3 SEO model: three filters for picking keywords

Cody's V3 SEO Model is a keyword selection framework. He developed it at Reynolds + Myers, where the team works with high-authority domains. One client's domain was older than google.com (21 years old at the time of writing) and had a backlink portfolio to match.

- On domains this powerful, he says "Google 'tells' us what it wants us to rank for."
- The three V's are **value**, **verge** and **volume**.
- A keyword that meets all three is called a **V3 keyword**. In his diagram, V3 keywords are the overlap of the three circles.

## Value: keywords that create value for the business

Cody's first filter is whether ranking for a keyword can create value for the organization.

- Keywords related to the company's products.
- Information prospects search for at different stages of the buyer's journey.
- Keywords important to brand identity.

Ranking for keywords that fail this filter produces traffic but no business impact, so they are excluded even if they rank easily.

## Verge: keywords already on page two or three

Cody's second filter is that the keyword is on the verge of page one.

- The keyword already ranks on page two or three of Google.
- He argues Google signals what a site can rank for. A keyword already showing up on pages two or three is one Google considers the site relevant for.
- These keywords "can often be lifted to page one with just a little work."
- This makes verge keywords the cheapest wins. It is incremental effort on existing rankings, not a new ranking built from zero.

## Volume: keywords big enough to move the needle

Cody's third filter is that the keyword has a monthly search volume that is relatively large for the industry.

- The keyword must have "the potential energy necessary to create impact to our current search analytics."
- Volume is judged relative to the industry, not as an absolute number.
- Without this filter, lifting a verge keyword to page one would barely register in clicks or impressions.

## Executing the model: build landing pages for V3 keywords

Cody's process once V3 keywords are identified:

1. Find keywords that satisfy value, verge and volume at the same time.
2. Build landing pages that best answer search intent for each new target keyword.
3. Wait about 30 days.

## Result: +51% organic clicks and +29% organic impressions in 30 days

Cody reports that applying the V3 model for a client at Reynolds + Myers produced these results within 30 days:

- 51% more organic clicks.
- 29% more organic impressions.

He notes the client had a very old, powerful domain, which is the context in which he says Google tells you what it wants you to rank for. He shows the result as a Search Console style chart in the original post and invites questions, saying he will answer "hastily."
