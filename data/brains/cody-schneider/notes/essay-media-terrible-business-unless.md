## Media is a vehicle for selling an owned product, not the product itself

- Cody calls media "a terrible business" if the revenue comes from selling ads.
- A friend who owns a big email newsletter makes great money, but "selling ads is a grind": constant outbounding for sponsors, managing advertiser expectations, and budgets that get cut overnight.
- Cody says every media business owner he has talked to says "the juice is just not worth the squeeze."
- His fix: sell a product you own through the media.
- Key takeaway in his words: "Media isn't the product. Media is the vehicle to sell something."

## Owned-media-plus-product examples (B2B and DTC)

- Cody cites Logan Paul doing Prime as proof: "ads don't make you a billionaire." He makes the same point about Jimmy (MrBeast) selling chocolate.
- **B2B example: Workweek.** It builds podcasts for B2B niches. Example: the Wolf of Franchises podcast sells Krokit.com to its audience. Cody says that from back channels he heard "they print."
- **DTC example: DearMedia.** It builds podcasts for consumers, then builds DTC brands on top of them. Example: The Skinny Confidential sells body shaping devices to its audience.
- Pattern: build the audience first (podcast, newsletter), then launch a product the audience already trusts you to recommend.

## Short-form LinkedIn: pick a format and let it rip

- Cody says short-form LinkedIn content is "still just going" and tells people to just do it.
- The tactic: find a format and repeat it consistently.
- He notes that when new content goes viral, it resurrects old content, so a back catalog keeps paying off.

## Turn webinars into long-tail SEO traffic with AI

Cody's workflow for getting more views on webinar recordings:
1. Get the transcript of the webinar.
2. Have AI list 20 long-tail keywords related to the webinar.
3. Have AI write a blog post for each keyword.
4. Post the blogs to your site and get them indexed.
5. Embed the webinar recording on each page.

His summary: "simple."

## Business ideas and distribution plans

- **YouTube thumbnail library subscription.** Distribution: blog posts targeting long-tail keywords. Also scrape YouTubers' emails from their About pages (he suggests using Fiverr for the scrape) and cold email 100,000 people a month.
- **AI job description writer.** Growth: an exact-match domain plus backlinks, and Google Ads on job description writer keywords.
