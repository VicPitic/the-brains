## Software activation mirrors a YouTube video: the promise, then the delivery

- Cody drew this from reviewing the signup flow with the engineering team of a seed-stage startup he was consulting for. He says YouTube videos and software activation to paid have "the same biology."
- A good YouTube video needs a thumbnail and title combination that earns the click. Then it must deliver on that promise as soon as possible so the viewer actually consumes the media.
- In software, the homepage is the thumbnail/title. It makes a promise.
- Immediately after signup, the product has to deliver on what the homepage promised. That delivery is what produces activation to paid.
- Activation is the user's "lightbulb moment." The goal is to reach it as fast as possible, so build the signup-to-activation flow to instigate that moment.
- The promise is an opportunity for trust. If it isn't delivered, it "erodes all trust instantly."

## Local SEO playbook: directory links plus an indexing bot

- Building the first foundational links for a local business is hard. Cody's answer is directory links.
- The problem: directory links don't get seen easily because the directory sites are huge. Example: Mapquest has 12M pages. An indexing tool is needed to get the linking pages seen.
- The process:
  1. Buy directory links on Fiverr.
  2. Submit the links the Fiverr seller provides to the SpeedyIndex Telegram bot for indexing.
- Result: he did this for a friend's website and got 118 links built in 7 days.
- SpeedyIndex is a Telegram bot that indexes links you built for your site.

## Personal newsletter growth: Twitter cadence, thread CTA and DM automation

- Cody decided to grow his newsletter and almost doubled it in 4 weeks, reaching about 100 new subscribers a day.
- The system:
  1. Post 70 tweets a week: 10 per day, one every hour from 7am to 5pm.
  2. Add a call to action to subscribe to the newsletter on every post, as a thread reply on a 10-minute delay.
  3. DM every new follower asking them to subscribe to the newsletter. He automated this entirely with Phantom Buster.
