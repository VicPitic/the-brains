## Newsletter values are heading toward zero, creating a consolidation opportunity

- Cody says a friend who owns one of the big AI newsletters told him he is "scooping up newsletters for next to nothing."
- His reasons for the collapse:
  - Beehiiv and Substack made starting a newsletter easier than ever, so the market got saturated.
  - Content is a grind. Most people can't make it sustainably, so burnout is imminent.
  - There is more ad inventory than people who want to buy it.
- His prediction: "This space is going to crash."
- The opportunity he sees is for brands to buy these newsletters cheaply and consolidate them.

## Creator-first founders: build in public becomes the norm

- Cody argues startups have become "a spectator sport." He says he wishes he had written that line first.
- About 200M people in the US want to start a business, and they want to watch and obsess over startups like Formula 1.
- Ride-alongs, build-in-public posts and vlogs of startup life are a "blue ocean strategy" and a differentiator.
- He says this gives founders leverage with customers, investors and marketing partnership negotiations.
- He predicts doing investor updates in public will become the norm.
- His summary: "Every company will be a media company. Start now or die on the vine."

## Generating 1,000 ads with AI (content he recommends)

Cody calls a video showing a creator's process of making 1,000 ads with AI for a client "one of the most insane things I've ever seen." His takeaways:
- Stack: Midjourney, DALL-E and a Figma workflow that builds thousands of display ad variations in seconds, both on-brand and unique in message.
- Customization: AI can adjust ads to specific locales or demographics, accounting for cultural and regional nuances, which increases effectiveness.
- Scaling: ad production scales without a proportional increase in resources, so businesses can run larger campaigns or more clients at once.
- Testing: AI makes testing ad variants "feel like nothing," so you can quickly find and deploy the best ads and improve ROI.
- Future: he expects more personalized, interactive and contextually relevant advertising.
