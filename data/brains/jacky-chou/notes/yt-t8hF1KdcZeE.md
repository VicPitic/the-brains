## Legacy media partnerships and rank-and-rent affiliate

- He partnered with dying legacy media outlets that have strong domain authority (e.g. a local newspaper site). He carved out an affiliate or supplement section on their domain and monetized hard.
- The pages flew to number one. Highest monthly affiliate income was about $550K.
- Sponsored rank-and-rent: for "best protein powder"-type keywords, he reached out to the brands and charged a flat fee plus a percentage to be listed at number one. The highest placement was $50K a month plus 50% commission.
- That stream is now "pretty much dead", at about $16-17K a month.
- He calls it "the most insane high" of his life.

## LLM SEO and Reddit manipulation

- He doesn't sell services he isn't highly certain about. He is testing LLM SEO ("GEO" in A16Z's term) and says results are not yet conclusive.
- Direction he points people toward: Reddit and Quora, which he says are easy to manipulate. The method:
  1. Google a commercial query such as "best FBA courses 2025".
  2. Find the Reddit thread that ranks.
  3. List your own offer in it from a proxy account.
  4. Upvote it massively and add fake supporting comments.
  5. Appearing in those threads gets you into LLM answers.
- Cost claims: about five cents per upvote, farmed accounts for a few dollars each, and subreddit mods can reportedly be bribed for around $1,000.
- It won't work in high-competition niches like supplements, VPN or mattresses.
- He sees a service gap: proactive reputation building on Reddit, as opposed to reactive removals.

## Rank-and-rent and local lead gen

- Core tactic: get the Google Business Profile location verified, "I don't care how you do it", and the calls start flowing, especially in tier two and tier three cities.
- Outside the US (Australia, Canada, UK, Germany) is even easier. He ranked a roofing keyword in Berlin in about two days as a case study for a friend, who then picked up a roughly $150K deal.
- Bridging trick: if a rank-and-rent site isn't ranking yet but you signed a client for $2K, put that $2K into paid ads while waiting for SEO. Cut spend as rankings rise.
- He sold towinglist.com, then the largest towing directory in the US, when a prospect he was selling to turned out to be sketchy and made subtle threats.
- A friend with a number of Google Business Profiles earns about $300-500 a day just sending calls through call networks. Going direct would earn more, but networks need no effort.
- He is interested in doing local SEO plus info coaching run at break-even to feed his software.

## Blogs, black hat, and Facebook traffic

- Blogs still work, but most people do it wrong. Blasting hundreds of AI posts a day gets you hit by Google's filters, and weak domains are "blacklisted immediately". Nobody recovers anymore.
- His approach for legitimate blogs: optimize with a tool like NeuronWriter (cheap on AppSumo) and publish only one or two posts a day.
- Black-hat approach: buy a strong expired domain at GoDaddy auctions (e.g. an old crypto project that had a billion-dollar market cap) and push heavy affiliate articles across supplements, pills and similar. Try to earn the money back in the first few days before Google catches on.
- Organic Facebook for display-ad blogs (e.g. a vegan recipe site):
  - Buy page likes at fractions of a cent each, using clickbait like-bait pages ("like if you love golden retrievers").
  - Post clickbaity content and never link in the first post; put the link in the thread.
  - Send traffic to the blog and run it in high-RPM geos (US, Canada).
  - He cites 1-10K a day on Mediavine-type display income if posts go viral.
