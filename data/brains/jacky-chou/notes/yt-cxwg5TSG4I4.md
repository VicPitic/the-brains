## Why Jacky doesn't build links to partnership sites

- All of Jacky's revenue now comes from partnerships (he has one or two other sources, such as marketing letter sponsorships, which he mostly doesn't take outside Black Friday).
- He has never built backlinks for any partner site. On a high-DR site, he says all you do is have AI write the content, have a human humanize it, post it, and it ranks.
- His workflow for partner sites is: post, interlink, and occasionally some CTR (not really needed).
- He argues content quality barely matters right now. Before Forbes got manually hit, he says "you could probably get a 2-year-old to write a post about best credit cards and it'll rank."
- He loses some respect for in-house SEOs at DR90+ sites, because ranking there is easy.
- Hard cases are competitive terms like "best CRM software" on a new DR30 site. He'd be impressed by an in-house SEO who ranked that, and says they'd need parasite SEO or partnerships.
- Instead of links, he puts effort into more partnerships, content for partners, and digital PR for partners.

## Why Jacky rarely builds links to his own sites

- He only builds links to sites he owns, and only about a year's worth of that.
- Most of his own sites are at zero traffic: de-indexed, not just down 50%. He says it's not backlinks. Causes he names: Google thinking he spammed the blog with AI content (some sites), and some caught together in the same Google Search Console account.
- The surviving sites grow slowly, around 3 to 4K organic visits a month, which he says isn't worth his time or link spend.
- He sees links as worthwhile in only four scenarios: rank and rent, parasite SEO, local links, and gambling-type plays (covered in the sections below).

## Jacky's link-building business and margins

- Jacky sells backlinks as one of his services: niche edits, local SEO backlinks, and local retainers. He says the variety isn't huge.
- Backlinks bring in about $30-40K a month.
- Gross margin is roughly 50%. He calls that low for link building, since most sellers run about 80% margins by marking up 4x. He marks up only 2x, and says that's what makes it worth it.

## Rank and rent: the order of operations before buying links

- Paid links (niche edits, guest posts) on a rank and rent site come only after the site hits roughly the top 10.
- Before any niche edits or guest posts, he follows his SOP (free on YouTube; a more succinct start-to-finish version is in the Indexsy community):
  1. Build citations.
  2. Tier the citations with a PBN. Do not point the PBN directly at the rank and rent site. He says 10+ people did this and then complained of ranking drops.
  3. Submit the citations to an indexer. He says most people skip this.
- He wants to drop CTR from the SOP because people overdo it. His example: someone ran 100 clicks a day on a keyword with about 100 local searches a month, got to the front page, and then got penalized.

## Parasite SEO, local links and gambling

- Parasite SEO: sending links to a page on a parasite domain makes Google value that page. With multiple pages on the same parasite domain, Google will show yours on the front page. He calls it very easy to abuse right now.
- Example: any Southeast Asian country can rank for gambling terms with a few links from the US. He says one of his biggest "benders" is in gambling.
- Local links: a local guest post, a naked backlink, plus map embeds. He calls this extremely effective and probably the best way to grow right now.

## Fake address setup for local rank and rent

- Jacky uses a fake address in the target city and adds schema markup.
- He picks an office building in the absolute center of the city.
- Reason: if he later wants to register a Google Business Profile, he can rent an office in that building, register legitimately, and open for business.
