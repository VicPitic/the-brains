## Where AI helps in the SEO workflow

Jacky frames AI as a complement to traditional SEO, not a replacement: "AI SEO improves and complements traditional SEO practices." Its main value is automating manual, repetitive work so the process is faster, more accurate and cheaper, which he says matters most for small businesses with limited resources.

- **Fewer manual errors:** AI tools reduce costly mistakes such as picking the wrong keywords, under-using a target keyword, or writing on the wrong topics.
- **Strategy input:** AI surfaces insights about which topics a site is most likely to rank for.
- **Time saved:** it automates topic generation, keyword research and content promotion.

## Using AI for keyword research

Jacky lists four ways AI speeds up keyword research:

- **Trends:** analyzes keyword data, spots the latest trends and suggests keywords likely to perform.
- **Suggestions:** proposes keywords you may not have considered.
- **Competition analysis:** finds less competitive keywords that are still relevant to the business.
- **Long-tail keywords:** identifies specific, relevant long-tail terms that drive targeted traffic.

## Using AI for link building

He names three link-building uses for AI tools:

- Analyze your site and find relevant link-building opportunities.
- Monitor your backlinks and recommend ways to improve their quality.
- Analyze competitors' backlinks and suggest new opportunities.

## Content generation and voice search

- Google's Search Quality Evaluator Guidelines say the highest-quality pages show the author's knowledge, authority and trustworthiness. Jacky ties content quality to that standard.
- Standards differ by content type: news should be in-depth, well-cited and accurate; artistic content original and unique; informational content comprehensive, accurate, clearly communicated and reflecting expert consensus.
- AI can automate parts of content work, such as improving internal linking or identifying authoritative articles on a topic.
- **Voice search (VSEO):** most voice searches are questions, so he recommends using AI tools to surface the questions searchers ask, then building content that answers them. He cites a stat that 57% of teenagers use voice search often, and a prediction that by 2030 voice searches will account for most consumer purchases.
- VSEO is "hypercompetitive" because voice assistants give one answer only, the one at the top of the SERPs.

## Prioritizing with analytics

AI-driven analytics can produce automatic insights on opportunities and anomalies, but people still decide which steps to take. Jacky's point: any SEO tool can recommend hundreds of low-value tasks, and it is more compelling to offer three recommendations that greatly boost the site's statistics.

## Limits of AI content

He lists three weaknesses of AI-generated SEO content:

- **Reduced creativity:** it is hard to get a human touch, so content can be less engaging and less unique.
- **Lack of context and depth:** AI can imitate a tone but not the nuance, empathy or conversational depth that make writing relevant.
- **Lack of innovation:** output is based on existing data, so it struggles to produce new ideas and can lack common sense.

## Will AI content rank, and what Google allows

- AI content can rank, but it needs human creativity and editing to be both engaging and search-friendly. AI tools are "not a substitute for human-written SEO content."
- Google doesn't ban AI content because of its origin. The problem is content that offers readers no value or is made to manipulate rankings.
- Per Google Search Central, spammy AI content includes: text that makes no sense to readers but contains SEO keywords; low-quality content with no reader value; AI content published without a human checker; and content with no concern for reader experience.
- His conclusion: publish meaningful, informative content and there is no reason for Google to penalize it.

## Review and personalize before publishing

Jacky's manual checklist for AI-generated drafts:

- **Spelling and grammar:** AI suggestions can introduce errors or change a statement's meaning.
- **Writing style:** humans add nuance based on how the audience connects (ad copy, for example, doesn't need perfect grammar).
- **Tone:** make the content match the brand's emotion and voice.
- **Structure:** check headings, subheadings and flow; Google also checks for proper structure.
- **Context:** make sure every paragraph ties to the main idea and flows together.

Then personalize with real expertise, since "your perspective and real-world experience are what give your content value":

- Link to reliable sources that support your claims.
- In product reviews, add images and tell the story of how you used the product.
- In ads and social captions, bring in brand voice, human emotion and the right personas.
