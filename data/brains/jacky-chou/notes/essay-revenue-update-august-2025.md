## Why Jacky diversifies revenue streams

- A year before this update, he was making $500k per month from affiliate and display ads (Amazon and Mediavine). Those sources are now under five percent of that former level.
- He argues that if he had stayed all in on Amazon and Mediavine, he would be struggling.
- New ventures LocalRank.so and Trackings.ai added $71k in August alone.
- His framing: "Diversification is insurance against algorithm changes and platform risk."
- Rule: "Do not rely on a single revenue source because algorithms can change overnight."

## Double down when something works

- LocalRank started about five months before the update and is already his largest revenue driver. Trackings launched three days before the post and was already moving fast.
- His rule: when he sees traction, he goes all in.
- He notes new projects can surpass old ones quickly. Five months earlier LocalRank did not exist.
- He teased another software project launching the next month, which he expected to be a "home run".

## Build in public as a distribution strategy

- Sharing revenue numbers attracts attention. One tweet about his revenue went viral and gained him over 1,000 followers in 24 hours.
- He argues transparency fuels trust and product launches.
- He says reporting wins publicly builds authority and audience faster than ads.
- He uses the monthly revenue post itself to cross-promote his products: LocalRank academy (get your first local SEO client, guaranteed), BrowserBlast (Indexsy's main revenue source) and the net worth tracker.

## Balancing long-term and short-term plays

Jacky splits his portfolio by role:
- STR Lisbon (short-term rental real estate): his "retirement plan", stable income.
- Software and SEO products: scalable growth.
- Smaller plays like hosting and affiliates: low-effort bonus revenue.

The mix across SEO products, SaaS, real estate and affiliates is what kept August only slightly down month over month.
