## Indexsy: link building and local guest posts

- Indexsy is part link building, part client work, effectively a holding company. Of October's revenue, $41k came from link building.
- Link building covers niche edits and, mainly, "local guest posts". He believes Indexsy is still the only one on the internet selling them.
- A local guest post is a single link (orders of 1 to 10 links) on a page that includes a citation and an embedded map, which is how it helps local SEO.
- He recommends choosing the dofollow URL type, because local guest posts are "actually safe to use".
- It is currently a best seller, outselling niche edits.
- His spin-off SEO agency: Jacky's team still handles the SEO, while his business partner handles account management. Prospects DM Jacky on Twitter and he introduces them.

## Amazon affiliate and Levanta

- Amazon affiliate made $38.5k in October. He calls that solid compared to most people but weak by his own history: his all-time high was around $100k months.
- He says it has been "clapped down heavily". Revenue comes from partnerships, and Google "seems to find all my partnerships at this point", yet he still manages to scrape by.
- Levanta matches affiliates with Amazon sellers, who then offer heightened commission rates, up to about 50% for him.
- He picks Levanta over FanFuel because it converts better. Pushing someone to a product with 4,000 Amazon reviews converts far better than sending them to sketchier sites. He still likes FanFuel and earns well from it.

## Community, parasite SEO and the Advise lift

- Advise is his private community. Most of its levers come from Jacky himself.
- He credits this month's jump to two changes:
  1. He built a "$2k a day" parasite SEO site in public inside the community's secret channel.
  2. He ran a paid parasite opportunity for Advise members only. Around a hundred-plus people took part, and several said they made a couple hundred dollars each. He admits it is modest, but says "it pays for your membership".
- The $2k-a-day site was live only about two weeks and made about $18k before getting hit.

## Income volatility, longevity and diversification

- He expects the adult revenue source to be very low next month because the $2k-a-day site got "clapped down", which shows how fragile this kind of revenue is.
- Media Vine went from about $100k a month to $2,400. Partners moved away and refused to pay a percentage. His fix was to create a partnership contract so partners cannot cut him out again.
- He argues making-money-online niches have poor longevity, so you should work in sprints: earn as much as possible in a short window, then invest aggressively in lower-risk assets. He has seen many niche site owners "completely rocked" recently.
- His Binance affiliate work produced his first million dollars. The many users he referred still pay out, which he calls "the Binance dividends".
- On the investing side, he prefers stocks over real estate and says portfolios need diversification. He considers himself overweight in real estate and has been moving heavily into equities over recent months.
- He agrees that hustling and saving aggressively when young, then putting money into index funds, is the easiest route to seven figures over a decade.
- His Lisbon short-term rental is an 8-unit building, bought for his Golden Visa, which has now gone through. Units are one bed plus a sofa bed, and he thinks he is undercharging per night.

## Revive: refreshing publish dates

- Revive is a WordPress plugin that refreshes an article's publish date. He says this is a strong Google signal, confirmed in the Google algo leak.
- It goes a step further by using AI to rewrite the first paragraph of each article, which makes the refresh more natural because the content is genuinely edited.
- It also submits the page to an indexer, though he was unsure whether the indexer was still working and said he would check.
