## Why Jacky says not to start an affiliate site in 2024

- His answer to "would you start an affiliate site in 2024" is "hell no."
- Reason: it is not profitable, and effort is not rewarded. Authentic content does not get rewarded as of now, though that may change.
- Example: Retro Dodo went from 500k a month in traffic to 30k a month and was "decimated."
- Google promised to fix its algorithm but has not. He says it is prioritizing high domain authority sites.
- Because of that, he says to seek partnerships. He adds that when you approach sites for partnerships, they "don't like it."
- His closing line: "whatever you do don't start an affiliate site in 2024, you're going to get clapped."

## Jacky's recommended path instead of affiliate sites

He lays out a sequence for people starting out:

1. Short-term parasite SEO (burn-and-churn) to make a first $50-$100 online.
2. Rank and rent sites.
3. Client work.
4. Partnerships for the long term.

Right now he says to focus on partnerships, client work and rank and rent. He mentions Cricas getting to about $10K a month, which he describes as client work.

## Short-term parasite SEO as a first-dollar tactic

- He says short-term parasite SEO will get you to "probably a couple grand."
- He thinks many people give up too quickly, and may start recommending burn-and-churn parasite SEO to beginners.
- Why: once you make your first dollar online, "something switches in your brain." He says that was true for him.
- Steps he suggests:
  - Create around 10 Reddit accounts and farm them up.
  - Leave Reddit comments and make money from them.
  - Create subreddits and make money from those.
  - Aim for $50-$100 first, to "get on the board" and see that it is possible.
- He says the paid parasite SEO tactic in his Indexsy community is working "extremely well right now."

## Rank and rent, then local client work

- Once you have a first grand from parasite SEO, build rank and rent sites.
- Example: his public physio rank and rent test in Vancouver, where he says he "only bought citations" and it ranked.
- After ranking, you rent the site out.
- His plan if doing physio in Vancouver:
  - Become "the physio marketing guy in Vancouver."
  - Take one client from each neighborhood.
  - Rank for all the suburbs to dominate the niche.
  - Become the local SEO agency for it.
- Income estimates (his confident estimates, not guarantees):
  - About $3-4K a month from rank and rent.
  - Easily over $10K, even up to $20K, from local client work in a single niche like physio.

## Long term: partnerships and content around a hobby

- Long term, he would focus on partnerships. Example: bring the ranked local asset to local newspapers and sign them up for a partnership.
- He is "pretty bullish" on YouTube right now. He says his retirement plan is probably to find a hobby and create content around it.
- He also says affiliate-style content around a topic on YouTube could be a way to do it, rather than a new affiliate site.

## Consistency beats most people

- Thought experiment: if posting a YouTube video every day for a year could replace your full-time salary, most people would say yes, but almost no one would stick with it.
- He says even he, at nearly a year of daily episodes (Day 342), skipped some days.
- He cites Harvard's intro programming course online: the first video has about 50 million views, and the second has roughly 5% of that. His read: "people just don't want to do work anymore."
