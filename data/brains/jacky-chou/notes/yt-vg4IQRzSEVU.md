## Revenue peak and drop: $506k/month to roughly $200k/month in a year

- In Nov 2023 Jacky hit $506k in monthly revenue, with year-to-date revenue at $2 million at that point. He sustained roughly $500k/month for about four months before "getting clapped" by a Google update.
- By Nov 2024 revenue had fallen about 60%, to around $200k/month. He says enough time has passed that he can discuss it without resentment toward Google.
- He compares each revenue source from a year earlier against the most recent month (see the breakdown below).
- Daily snapshot on the day before the video: $2,260 total revenue (up $60 from the day before). Amazon $982, Mediavine $116, and the adult revenue source $1,166.

## Revenue source breakdown: then vs. now

Figures are monthly, a year ago vs. last month, as Jacky reports them:
- **Advice (his SEO/marketing product):** $16.5k → $34.4k. The one source that grew a lot, helped by annual billings from Black Friday/Cyber Monday deals.
- **Rank and rent (retail-heavy):** $125k a year ago. He does not give the current figure clearly.
- **Mediavine:** $119k gross a year ago, of which his cut was only about $29k, because he took 20% of a $103k pool coming from partner domains.
- **Amazon Associates:** $113k → about $38k. He attributes the peak to heavy partnerships.
- **Indexsy:** $94.6k a year ago, much of it from newsletter sponsorships. He says most Advice and Indexsy sales come from his newsletter.
- **Lisbon short-term rental:** $15k → $9k, and he expects this month to dip.
- **Fan Fuel:** now only about $1k/month.
- **Smaller lines mentioned:** a VPN source, a bonds-related source, and a crypto-related "moon bag" holding. He calls one a 10-bagger and notes the bull market is back.

## What drove the $500k peak: partnerships and going all-in

- Jacky says he identified "the power of partnerships," meaning leveraging other people's domains, and pushed it harder than people would believe.
- The team was "firing on all cylinders":
  - Bulk keyword research, because of the large team.
  - Heavy formatting work and daily article optimization checks.
  - Constant outreach.
  - Articles being rented out as soon as they were fixed.
- Takeaway: the peak came from one scalable lever (renting and partnering on other people's domains) plus massive team execution, which also made it fragile to a single Google change.

## Sprint on what works

- Jacky's core rule: when a test hits, "you have to Sprint." Normal pace is walking and "smelling the flowers," but a working channel gets everything.
- Example: if he finds something working on a Saturday morning, he cancels weekend plans and puts the whole team on it.
- Example: Reddit comments. After he told his audience it worked, he says around five people made over $10k/month doing it.
- He frames this as setting yourself up to monetize luck: some luck is involved, but you position to capture it fast.

## Testing over opinions

- "You have to be always testing." He says not to take anyone's word on what works or doesn't, because most are "SEO philosophers."
- Run tests properly, and once something hits, push hard (see the sprint rule above).
- He reports his results publicly, including declines, and says the numbers he posts are not lies. He tracks everything in a "Building in Public" reference sheet.

## Tactics and offers mentioned

- **Black Friday plan for Advice:** a deal in November, then a price hike right afterwards. He describes dropping the price and then raising it back up, on top of prices that already go up every month.
- **Sponsorships:** he took one newsletter sponsorship for Black Friday this year and says the marketing newsletter is a huge converter. He notes he probably should take more.
- **Citations in foreign markets:** in answer to a question about non-US citations (e.g. Germany), he says the citations tactic works abroad. Buy the market-specific citation package, such as the German one. He says it works better in foreign markets, and that backlinks work well there too.
- **Channel membership:** $5/month, with proceeds going to charity.
