## Jacky's $1M+ SEO loss in 2024: what was lost

- In a Day 331 build-in-public episode, Jacky says he lost over $1 million of site value in 2024 to Google updates. His reasoning: "if you can report the highs you got to report the lows as well."
- He estimates he spent about $400k on niche sites. All of them are at zero or effectively zero now. He says that in hindsight he should have sold most of his holdings the year before.
- Farway, his ecom brand, was the first big hit:
  - He built it from $0 to $20k/month in 3 months.
  - He valued niche sites at roughly 3x monthly revenue (annualized as 3x of $20k x 12 is not stated; he says about "$700k" of value).
  - It was hit in April and went to zero.
  - Its Mediavine revenue was around $6.7k-$7.8k (he says "67 8K" in the transcript, so the figure is unclear), with a lot of informational content.
- Several other niche sites he owned alone were hit with it, at roughly $500 to $15-20k/month each. That was about another $700k of value gone.
- Total value lost on these sites: about $1.4 million.

## Mistake: sites in one Google Search Console account

- Jacky put Farway and several other niche sites in the same Google Search Console account. When Farway was hit, the others went down with it.
- His reasoning at the time: his partnerships were making about $500k/month, so $20k/month of niche sites didn't matter to him. His priority was "as long as you don't clap my 500k site."
- Lesson he draws: don't put assets of different value or risk in the same Search Console account.

## Mistake: talking publicly about a site's revenue

- Jacky posted Farway revenue and Mediavine screenshots openly while it was growing. He says that in hindsight this was stupid.
- What he would do differently: stop talking about a site once it passes certain revenue thresholds. If people ask, he can discuss it, but he would not post proactively. His summary: "I think I just should have just shut up when money was good."
- He ties the sites' fall to the attention from posting, and says about $1.4M of value "went down the drain for no reason" beyond online clout.

## The trade-off: public posting still paid off

- Jacky says posting openly about Farway got him his first partnership deals.
- Those partnerships grew to a couple million in profit in 2024.
- A snowball effect followed: he kept talking about partnerships, became "the partnership guy," and new brands came to him with deals.
- His conclusion is that he did most things right, and that posting probably earned him more than he lost. He still wishes he had stopped talking about Farway after January.
- One side benefit he sees: because he posted so openly, nobody doubts the numbers.

## Recovery and a new public build

- Jacky says he has several new sites. Recovery of the affected ones is in progress, but he won't say which sites or what he is doing.
- Applying his lesson, he built a new site in public inside his Indexsy community (advise community). It went from $0 to $200/day, and then to $1,800/day in two months.
- He was reporting it from zero, but at $1,800/day he says "if I lose this it'll hurt," and he worries about leaks to Google.
