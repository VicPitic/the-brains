## Rank and rent beyond local SEO

- Jacky says rank and rent is not limited to local keywords. It does not have to be "accountant Vancouver"; it can be a broad national keyword such as "best protein supplement".
- He says he signed five-figure clients by ranking his own site for national keywords like that.
- Local keywords are still much easier to rank for, so local is the easier place to start.
- The model he describes: build a site that ranks and generates leads, own the asset, then get paid by a business for the leads it produces, instead of the business paying to own the site.

## Site structure and page-building tactics

- A single page can rank in low-competition niches and cities. Jacky ranked "physio in East Vancouver" on a single-page site, which he showed on his YouTube channel.
- He does not recommend limiting yourself to one page. With AI, you can screenshot a reference site structure, paste it into Claude, and have it generate a whole site in one shot.
- Build service pages and suburb or location pages, such as "payroll services Round Rock", even when keyword plus city shows low volume.
- Reason: people searching "payroll services near me" from that location will surface those pages. So he says to check "near me" volume, not just keyword-plus-city volume, and to create the suburb pages anyway.
- He says he "used to be a non-believer" in this approach but changed his mind after seeing it work.

## Link building caution

- Jacky is particular about never pointing tier 2 links directly at the target URL, because the site will "collapse". Tier 2 links point at the citations instead.

## Finding and managing partners

- He expects to cycle through a lot of partners. "Most local business owners are pretty trash at running a business." They are good at their trade, not at running a company, and many can barely hold it together.
- The tactic is to keep trying partners ("pepper your angles"). Once you find a dependable one, "hold on for dear life" and charge them accordingly so everyone wins.
- Meta ads are a method he taught for finding partners. Run a simple funnel with an ad asking "Do you want more leads?". Those who say yes fill out which service they provide. This gives a steady inflow, but the partners are often not dependable.

## Google Business Profile

- Jacky calls a Google Business Profile "super super super valuable". He says it makes the economics work about 10 times better and makes leads far easier to get, especially in your own city.
- Click-through rate assumptions of 25-30% for position one apply to keywords that are not bid on in Google Ads. Realistically it is closer to half of that without a profile.
- With a Google Business Profile, he says you can go back to roughly a 30% click-through rate.
- His advice: if you can get one, get one.

## Pitching partners in person

- Show proof in person, for example your rankings.
- Cold calling and walking in are "like a muscle". You will get rocked the first few times, so get your reps in and you will get far better.
