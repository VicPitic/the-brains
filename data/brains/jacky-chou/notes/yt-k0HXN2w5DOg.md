## Timeline from $0 to about $500k/month (2014 to 2024)

Jacky says Indexsy and his other ventures hit roughly $500k in a month in late 2024. His path:

- **2014:** Failing courses, so he took a year off for a paid internship in Berlin at €450/month with €300 rent. He ate canned bouillon plus two eggs daily for about 3 months to save money.
- **2015:** Back in Canada finishing his degree, working part-time at GNC (supplement store) for roughly 1,500 CAD/month.
- **2016:** First job out of college as a growth manager at a hardware startup (Contact IO) for €3,000/month. He started Indexsy, an SEO agency, the same year. First client paid $1K/month; total income reached roughly $4,500/month.
- **2017:** Head of growth role at a Berlin startup for €6,000/month. Indexsy had 2-3 clients at about $2K/month each. Total roughly $8,500/month pre-tax.
- **2018:** Dropshipping peak, averaging about $55k/month for his split, with the agency also doing well.
- **2019-2020:** Dropshipping collapsed. Profit margin fell from 60-80% to about 20-30%. Indexsy "kept the lights on", averaging about $44.5k/month.
- **2021-2022:** The "Covid run". 16-hour days, crypto and GameStop trading, flipping sites and watches. The agency did about $200k/month and ecommerce about $150k/month.
- **2023:** Niche sites were hit by Google updates. He exited many beforehand, including a couple of six-figure exits. February revenue bottomed at $74k with profit of roughly $10-20k/month. The year averaged $222k/month; November and December were the two best months, at roughly $5xxk and $483k.
- **2024:** About $500k in the most recent month.

## Dropshipping: what worked and why it died

- **Start:** His partner Albert proposed it. They put in $5K each and burned about $99,500 before one product clicked.
- **Winner:** Cutlery sourced from China, peaking around $250k/month in revenue.
- **Why it collapsed (2019-2020):**
  - Facebook cracked down on dropshipping. After each purchase pixel fired, buyers got a quiz asking if they liked the product. Since the product hadn't arrived, they answered negatively, and enough negatives got the Facebook page and ad account banned.
  - The iOS update removed much purchase data.
  - Covid plus supplier problems: suppliers couldn't deliver for about a year after he paid $40-50k up front, tying up cash for a year and a half.
- **Lesson:** Revenue figures hid the damage. Going from $55k to $45k a month looked small, but margins went from 60-80% down to 20-30%.
- **Rescue:** Indexsy's agency income kept him afloat.

## Recovery: refocus on the agency and partnerships

- After the February 2023 low ($74k revenue, $10-20k profit), he concentrated on the agency side.
- He then "stumbled upon partnerships", which he says drove huge revenue into the business and took 2023 to a $222k monthly average.
- Selling niche sites before algorithm updates preserved value. He exited a lot of them before Google hit.
- Diversified income (agency, ecommerce, site flips, affiliate) let one business carry him while another failed.

## Partnership SEO model: how Jacky works with site owners

- **Access needed:** WordPress admin login on a subdirectory of the partner's domain (e.g. partnersite.com/advisor). He just needs a blank WordPress install there.
- **Content fit:** He prefers content relevant to the partner site's theme. They have stretched (home-ware sites pushing supplements) and it worked, but how far to stretch depends on the partner relationship.
- **Amazon affiliate:** Set up the account yourself, run it through your own tracking tag, and add the partner's site to your site list ("don't get clapped").
- **Optimization and authority:** SEO includes optimization; content still needs it. The partner's authority (e.g. a 10-year-old DR 63 domain) helps ranking but doesn't replace it.
- **OnlyFans:** They rank for OnlyFans keywords and push the traffic to an agency. He believes it's one of the best monetization methods in the space and is open to partnering with owners of OnlyFans content.
- **Links:** He vouches for a PBN seller whose links show up in Ahrefs.

## Rank and rent and parasite SEO tips

- **Google Business Profiles:** Can be bought, but he advises against it since it's illegal and people have been sued, though they do rank.
- **Citations:** Buy them for about $5 rather than building manually. He values his time more.
- **Lead forms:** A simple Contact Form 7 form emailed to you is enough.
- **Parasite posts:** Make as many as you want (e.g. "best massage therapy", "best kinesiology") redirecting to the same few rank and rent sites. The limit is budget, since parasite placements aren't free.
- **Traffic for a quiz or knowledge site:** He suggests trying Reddit comments.
- **YouTube automation channels:** He doesn't like them and recommends something more authentic, as he thinks YouTube is moving that way.
