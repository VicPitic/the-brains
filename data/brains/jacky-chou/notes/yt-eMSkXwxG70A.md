## How Jacky ranks online business models for 2026

- Jacky ranks business models on a tier list built with tiermaker.com. The tiers run from "scalable", "pretty reliable" and "decent money" down to "kind of saturated", "risky" and "basically a scam".
- He says he has "started and tried almost every single business model online."
- His overall pattern: agencies and B2B software rank high. Content-, trading- and funnel-based models rank low. Anything that depends on a platform's payouts or a commoditized service ranks low.
- Most of his placements are quick gut calls, and he often changes them while placing the next item. Treat them as opinions, not researched rankings.

## Agencies: his top-ranked category

- He says any agency is better than affiliate marketing in 2026. He puts agencies in "pretty reliable" or "decent money", not "scalable".
- A general online marketing agency is "pretty reliable" but too general. Specific agency types rank separately:
  - Google Ads agency: if it's local ads, he ranks it above a Facebook ads agency.
  - Facebook ads agency: if you get good at it, it sits above affiliate marketing.
  - Shopify agency: counts as an agency, so "decent money".
  - Web designer for local businesses (WordPress and similar): ranks above a social media management agency (SMMA), because you can resell the work.
  - Software development agency: you can white label it, but he says it "is going to zero". Probably saturated but still decent.
  - Social media management agency: a 2020-era model, but he says you can still make money if you can sell.
- Lead generation (rank and rent) is "decent money". It's an easy sell, and all you need is leads.
- Freelancing (web developer, SEO, etc.) is "decent money". He compares a freelancer to a mercenary: you do whatever the client needs.

## LLM/AI search agency: his favorite new bet

- He says an "LLM agency" (selling visibility in AI search) deserves a high placement in 2026. He ranks it above ads and lead gen.
- His claim: "LLM is the new SEO", and he says it converts very well as a sales pitch.
- Tactic: when an SEO prospect comes in, he throws in LLM optimization because it is "basically pretty similar." He says that closes them: "we do that and it's game over. Like they're 100% in the door."
- He adds that he can sell it this way only because he knows SEO himself. An SEO agency is essentially sold as an LLM agency now.

## Software, platforms and content businesses

- B2B SaaS (online tools): "pretty reliable". He says it's hard without a brand, so he places it a little below the very top.
- Platforms and marketplaces: the upside is "crazy" once both sides of the market exist, but they are kind of saturated. He ranks them above reselling sneakers.
- YouTube: he calls it the best platform, but only if you will grind. Posting daily puts it high. Posting about one video a month drops it to "risky but possible".
- Building a brand on social media: "pretty reliable" if you're willing to grind.
- Influencer or lifestyle creator: kind of saturated. He ranks it above affiliate marketing and above ecom, as long as you know how to monetize. He notes AI influencers are now a factor.
- Online courses: "risky but possible". He notes AI can generate full courses now, and he still ranks courses above affiliate marketing, influencer work and reselling sneakers.
- Online guru (course seller): "risky but possible", and pretty saturated. He jokes it's "funny coming from me."

## Saturated or declining models

- Affiliate marketing in 2026: kind of saturated. Any agency beats it.
- Ecom: kind of saturated, and he says plainly "I hate ecom." Dropshipping is in the same tier, and he ranks it below ecom.
- FBA (Amazon): pretty saturated, but he places it above ecom.
- Tech support or managed WordPress services: hard to sell and "so commoditized nowadays". He ranks them below black hat.
- Black hat SEO: "risky but possible". He ranks it above live streaming.
- Compilation videos: "risky but possible". He says the RPM is "pretty trash". You can make money but not a lot.
- Live streaming: "pretty trash", and he ranks it below garage sales.
- Esports: risky, but above garage sales.
- Reselling sneakers, eBay flipping: "risky but possible". You can make decent money but it is a job.
- Blogging in 2026: "Hell no", basically a scam.
- Graphic designer: "Hell no". With AI available, he says you're better off in another model.

## Models he calls scams

- Forex (foreign exchange): "definitely a scam".
- Day trading: "pretty much a scam", but he ranks it better than ClickFunnels.
- ClickFunnels: basically a scam.
- Crypto: risky.
- Online surveys: "100% a scam". He says you'd be better off getting a job at McDonald's.
- Adult content: he ranks it kind of saturated, below affiliate marketing, and says you're "selling your soul." This is his opinion, though one of his own revenue lines is labeled "the adult revenue source."
