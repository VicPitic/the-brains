## FeedHive revenue and growth milestone (Nov 2021)

- Simon announced that FeedHive, a 9-month-old SaaS, had crossed $65K in total revenue.
- FeedHive is an AI-driven social media marketing tool.
- He entered a "very well-established market" with it, which is why the growth felt significant to him.
- He treats growing to $65K+ in revenue as a win against established competition.

## FeedHive traction snapshot at 9 months

- MRR: $8,500.
- Monthly growth rate: around 20%.
- Users: more than 3,000 total.
- Paying users: around 600 on a paid plan.
- Total revenue at the time: more than $65K, nine months after launch.
- Rough conversion from the figures given: about 600 paid out of 3,000+ users, around one in five.

## Growth projection and target

- Simon's stated target: if the roughly 20% monthly growth rate holds, FeedHive passes $50K MRR when entering 2023.
- He frames this as conditional and uncertain: "Let's see."
- The projection is a straight extrapolation of the current monthly growth rate, not a committed plan.

## Product direction: AI and new features

- Simon says the team is "working hard on adding new features and using cutting-edge technology" to give users "superpowers" on social media.
- Product positioning leans on AI, including an analysis of 100,000 tweets used to predict how well a tweet will perform (published as a separate post titled "We used AI to analyze 100,000 tweets and can now predict how well your tweet will do").
- The bet is that AI features differentiate FeedHive in a crowded social media tool market.

## Building in public as a distribution channel

- Simon shares revenue, MRR, user counts and growth rate openly on Indie Hackers.
- The post is a milestone update with concrete numbers rather than a pitch, and it links to the product and to a related AI-analysis post.
- It targets the indie hacker community, which doubles as an audience for early SaaS users and credibility.
