## Link price is flat from DR 30 to DR 59, so pay for traffic and relevance, not DR points

- Ilias's catalog covers 10,030 sites (as of 30 Sept 2026), built from publisher email replies (4,734 sites) and reseller price lists (6,885 sites).
- Median guest post on a DR 30+ site: $100. Average: $470, pulled up by a few expensive news sites.
- Median is about $90 at every tier from DR 30 to DR 59, then $140 at DR 60-69, $395 at DR 70-79, and $899 at DR 80+.
- Price tracks traffic as much as DR. Median rises from $70 for sites under 100 visits/month to $743 for sites over 250,000.
- Across DR 30+ sites, log price correlates 0.49 with log traffic and 0.45 with DR. Publishers price on both, traffic slightly more.
- 49% of DR 30+ sites with at least 1,000 monthly visits ask $150 or less.
- His advice: "Do not pay DR 60 prices for DR 50 sites." Pay more for traffic and relevance, not a few DR points.
- He uses medians rather than averages because a few very expensive sites skew the mean.

## Check organic traffic before buying, because DR can be bought

- 43% of DR 30+ sites get under 1,000 organic visits/month; 24% get under 100.
- 31% of DR 50+ sites get under 1,000 visits/month.
- Median traffic by tier: DR 30-39 is 232/month, DR 50-59 is 1,132, DR 60-69 is 4,423, DR 80+ is 284,487.
- DR measures a site's own backlinks, and those can be bought too.
- His rule: "Check organic traffic before you buy, not only DR." A DR 55 site with 200 visits/month is not a DR 55 link in practice.

## Skip news sites unless needed, and expect restricted niches to cost more

- News sites charge a median of $1,100 per guest post, about 11 times the $100 median of other DR 30+ sites.
- Casino, CBD, and other restricted topics cost a median of 1.33x the normal price on the same site (1,389 site/reseller pairs).
- Only 128 of 10,030 sites (1.3%) offer nofollow links only; 96% of publishers who state link type offer dofollow.

## Link insertions are usually not cheaper than guest posts

- On 5,735 sites quoting both prices, 49% charge the same for an insertion as for a new guest post, and 38% charge less.
- The median insertion price equals the median guest post price.
- His read: many publishers charge for the link, not the article.
- He points to niche edits as the case where an insertion is the better choice.

## Compare resellers: the same site can cost twice as much

- 1,135 sites appear on two or more reseller price lists.
- The priciest reseller asks a median of 1.44x the cheapest for the same guest post on the same site. Median gap: $40 per link.
- On 49% of these sites the highest price is at least 50% above the lowest; on 30% it is 2x or more.
- His advice: "If you buy from link lists, compare more than one seller."

## What publishers expect: terms to plan for

Based on 4,057 publishers who stated at least one term (percentages count only those who stated that term):

- **Payment timing:** 84% want payment before publication, 16% after. His advice: pay only publishers you have checked.
- **Payment method:** 68% PayPal, 8% crypto, 7% bank transfer or Wise, 18% other.
- **Permanence:** 76% permanent, 24% time-limited. He advises asking for permanent links in writing.
- **Who writes:** 73% expect the buyer to supply the article, 21% either, 6% the publisher writes. Budget writing time or use a service that includes it.
- **Turnaround:** 89% publish within two days of receiving the article, 11% within a week.
- About 7% signal they reject AI-written articles.

## Publisher prices vs managed link building (LinkDR)

- Publisher and reseller prices cover only the placement. They exclude finding the site, checking traffic, pitching, negotiating, writing the article, paying up front, and chasing disappearing links.
- LinkDR (his product) prices per link: $160 for DR 30+, $280 for DR 40+, $400 for DR 50+, $500 for DR 60+.
- Included: publisher fee, outreach, a new article, payment, live and dofollow checks, a full refund if the link isn't live in 60 days, and a free replacement if removed within 12 months.
- Most links go live in 3-4 weeks.
