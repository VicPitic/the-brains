## Bank.Green case: strategy was a small set of high-intent keywords

Ilias's agency, MagicSpace SEO, worked with Bank.Green (a non-profit that helps people move their money to banks that don't fund fossil fuels) in its early stages. The brief covered four things at once:
- A digital growth strategy.
- A website that attracts and converts.
- Brand identity and positioning.
- Rankings for high-value keywords such as "green banking" and "eco banks".

He frames the SEO goal as mission-driven. Rank for what eco-minded people search, so they switch banks and put consumer pressure on traditional banks to stop financing fossil fuels.

## Keyword focus: quality over quantity

Ilias's stated takeaway is to avoid chasing thousands of keywords. Target a small set of highly relevant, high-intent terms instead, which in this case produced better rankings and more qualified traffic. The target terms were:
- "green banking"
- "eco banks"
- "sustainable banking"
- "is my bank green"

Bank.Green reached position #1 for all of them. Volumes reported: "green banking" at 350 monthly searches and "eco banks" at 200. These are small volumes, and the case shows that ranking first on niche, high-intent terms is a viable strategy.

## Combining brand, site and SEO from day one

For an early-stage organization, Ilias bundled positioning, the website and the SEO strategy together rather than treating them separately.
- Brand identity: a cohesive visual and messaging strategy aimed at environmentally conscious consumers, which he argues helps a young organization stand out in a competitive space and build trust.
- Website: built to be user-friendly and optimized for both user experience and search engines. He notes the site still earned compliments years after launch.

## Non-profits can win with SEO

Ilias argues that non-profits can get significant SEO results with the right strategy and execution. Ranking #1 for terms like "green banking" lets a mission-driven organization reach people already looking to make responsible banking choices, amplifying the mission without paid acquisition.
