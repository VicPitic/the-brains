## StoryChief engagement: scope and approach

Ilias, through MagicSpace SEO, ran a 6-month SEO and content marketing overhaul for StoryChief. StoryChief is an all-in-one content marketing platform (team collaboration, content creation, multi-channel distribution). It competed against Ahrefs, SEMrush, Backlinko and Neil Patel, and wanted more organic traffic and leads with less reliance on expensive PPC.

The engagement covered eight workstreams:
- High-quality content creation
- On-page and technical SEO optimization
- Strategic backlink acquisition
- Conversion rate optimization
- Video strategy improvements
- Affiliate marketing campaign enhancements
- Email nurture sequence refinement
- Weekly SEO consulting calls

Example content produced for StoryChief:
- "10 Best Screenshot Apps for Mac in 2024 (Free & Paid)"
- "Programmatic SEO Examples That Actually Work"
- "7 Powerful ChatGPT Prompts to Create SEO Content Faster"

## StoryChief results after 6 months

- Organic search traffic grew 6x, from 8,000 to 44,000 monthly clicks.
- Significant boost in high-authority backlinks and organic traffic to key landing pages.
- 80% of leads now come through SEO.
- PPC was eliminated. Previous CPA on PPC was over $2,000+.
- Content distribution strategy improved overall.

## Ilias's argument for SEO over PPC

- He frames SEO as the sustainable, long-term, cost-effective alternative to PPC for a B2B SaaS company.
- The shift from PPC to SEO "dramatically reduced customer acquisition costs": StoryChief went from $2,000+ CPA on paid to getting 80% of leads from organic.
- Even in a highly competitive market dominated by well-known SEO brands, a smaller player can compete by executing SEO well.

## Holistic SEO: combine content, technical and off-page work

- Ilias credits the result to a "multi-faceted SEO strategy" rather than any single tactic.
- Content, technical/on-page SEO, and off-page work (backlinks) were run together, alongside conversion rate optimization, video, affiliate and email nurture.
- Traffic alone was not the goal: conversion optimization and nurture sequences were part of the package so organic visits turned into leads.
- A weekly consulting call kept the strategy tuned over the 6 months.
