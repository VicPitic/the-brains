## Auditing platforms at scale: scrape customer sites and fix patterns once

Ilias and Sasha (MagicSpace) did a full technical audit for Framer, a platform with 32 people on the team and 1000+ customer sites. The audit covered Framer.com and its top customer sites.

- Ilias argues against checking sites one by one: "Scrape 1000 sites. Find patterns. Fix problems for everyone at once."
- Week one was research. They analyzed the top 1000 Framer sites, tracked issues in a spreadsheet, and used Screaming Frog SEO Spider to scrape every domain.
- Platform-wide fixes beat custom work per site. Framer needed improvements that helped thousands of customers, and broad solutions scale better.
- The approach suits SaaS platforms, website builders, and tools with many customer sites.
- Ilias frames Framer's core product as SEO, so being SaaS founders himself positioned them to suggest product improvements that help every Framer customer rank better.

## Common technical SEO blockers found across 1000 sites

The scrape surfaced the same problems across customer sites:

- Missing H1 tags on 25% of sites.
- Canonicalization problems across domains.
- Nofollow issues hurting crawl budget.
- Lorem ipsum still live on customer sites.
- Duplicate content.
- Staging domains getting indexed.
- Truncated titles.

Ilias's takeaway is that these are platform-level defaults and templates, so they can be fixed once in the product rather than site by site.
