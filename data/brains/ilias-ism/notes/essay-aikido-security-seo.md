## Aikido Security's one-month SEO engagement: results and framing

- Ilias's agency worked with Aikido Security when it was just 5 founders with a solid product that nobody could find in search.
- The engagement lasted one month, and Ilias says that was enough.
- Ilias says Aikido now has 100+ employees and generates "billions in revenue", crediting the work with the founders at the foundation stage.
- The outcome was #1, #2 and #3 rankings for their top keywords in that one month, without writing hundreds of new articles.
- Ilias frames the goal as an "SEO foundation they could build on", chosen together with the client, rather than ongoing content volume.

## Improve and restructure existing content before writing new

- Ilias's first step is to look at every blog post the company already has and improve every article found.
- He then does a complete keyword restructure, looking at all of the company's top keywords and making sure the existing content gets into the results for them.
- His rule: "Improve existing content first." The aim is to make existing content rank, not to produce volume.
- The reasoning is that a young startup has no time to write hundreds of articles, so effort goes where it already has assets.

## Using other people's listicles and articles to reach the top three fast

- Ilias does comprehensive backlink and listicle research: finding existing listicles and articles that rank for the target keywords.
- Getting the company into those third-party listicles and articles puts it in the top results immediately, instead of waiting for its own pages to rank.
- He argues this beats writing lots of content or wasting time: "use other listicles and articles to get into top results immediately."
- The focus is the results that matter, meaning the pages that already occupy the search results for the company's top keywords.

## Backlinks from your own pages

- Ilias writes a few strategic articles and adds backlinks from his own agency's pages.
- The tactic is to leverage existing authority by writing pages that feature the client company, which he says boosts domain authority.
- He combines this with the listicle placements rather than relying on either alone.

## Focus on top keywords and know when the foundation is enough

- Ilias's approach: look at all the top keywords, make sure the company appears in the results for them, and acquire #1, #2 and #3 spots for what matters rather than spreading effort widely.
- A small number of strategic articles, not a large content programme, completed the work.
- He argues one month can be enough to build the SEO foundation for an early-stage B2B SaaS, and that the stopping point should be decided together with the client.
- His agency's pitch is results "in weeks, not months", combining technical SEO with strategic content placement for B2B SaaS companies.
