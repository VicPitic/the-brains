## The 5-step landing page formula and why Ilias copies it across products

- Ilias frames the "perfect landing page formula" as a simple, repeatable process for building a high-converting landing page.
- The core of the page is three parts, written in this order: headline, description, bullet point benefits.
- He calls the bullet points "the most important part of the landing page". They summarize the product and its benefits in a way that is easy to understand, easy to remember and easy to scan, because "most people don't read every word on a landing page. They scan it to see if it's worth their time."

## Headline, description and bullets: what each part must do

- **Headline:** very short, simple to understand, concise, and shows the value and the unique selling point (USP).
- **Description:** specific numbers, benefits, reasons why, and the mission.
- **Bullet points:** 3-5 bullets, each with numbers and key points. Each starts with an emoji and states a concrete outcome.
- He packages this as a prompt for an AI "Conversion GPT": give it the formula and three worked examples, fill in your product name, and ask it to write "short, award-winning copywriting" in the order headline, description, bullets.

## Worked example: Nomad List

Ilias uses Pieter Levels' pages as the reference pattern.
- Headline: "Go nomad and work remotely around the world"
- Description: "Join the #1 global community of 33,106 remote workers living and traveling around the world since 2014" (a specific member count and a founding year as proof).
- Bullets:
  - Attend 433 meetups/year in 100+ cities
  - Meet new people for dating or making friends
  - Research destinations and find your best place to live
  - Keep track of your travels and record where you've been
  - Join Nomad List chat and find your community on the road

## Worked example: Interior AI and Photo AI

The same shape repeats, with an "instead of hiring an expensive professional" description.
- **Interior AI**
  - Headline: "Redesign your interior in seconds using AI"
  - Description: "Save money and use AI to redesign your interior from your laptop or phone instead of hiring an expensive interior designer"
  - Bullets: photo to AI redesign in seconds; choose a style (Modern, Minimalist, Contemporary); turn sketches and SketchUp files into photorealistic renders; Virtual Staging AI for empty homes in real estate; turn renders into 3D flythrough videos.
- **Photo AI**
  - Headline: "Create beautiful AI photos and videos without a camera"
  - Description: "Save money and use AI to do a photo shoot from your laptop or phone instead of hiring an expensive photographer"
  - Bullets: upload selfies to create your own AI character; take 100% AI photos in any pose, place or action; create 100% AI videos from any AI photo; run photo packs like AI Yearbook and Old Money; create AI-generated fashion designs with Sketch2Image™.
- Pattern: the headline names the outcome, the description gives the cost or time saving versus the human alternative, and the bullets list concrete features and use cases.

## Reuse one proven landing page across products

Ilias embeds Pieter Levels' account of copying one landing page style across his products:
- Pieter copy-pasted the Photo AI and Interior AI landing page style onto Nomad List, and conversion went "from 1% to almost 4%".
- The lineage: Interior AI v2's page is a copy of Photo AI's page, which is a copy of Nomad List's page from a year earlier.
- Added elements borrowed from a heavily A/B-tested page (Tabs Sex Chocolate by @oliver__b1): press mentions and testimonials.
- Added the bullet point benefits list popularized by @NapierHolland.
- Sign-up and login are unified into one email box. A new user gets an account created after payment; an existing user logs in.
- Every added or changed element is measured with Simple Analytics event funnels, so what works and what doesn't is visible quickly.
- Having the same page across 3 projects saves time; Remote OK was next.
- Ilias presents this as the proof that a tested landing page structure is worth copying and reusing rather than redesigning each time.
