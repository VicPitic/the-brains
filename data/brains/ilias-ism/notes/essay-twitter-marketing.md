## Why X is worth a solo founder's time

Ilias calls X "the fastest place to build an audience as a founder."

- The feed rewards people who "post consistently, reply a lot, and share what they are building," and one good post can reach far beyond your followers.
- Buyers there (founders, developers, marketers, investors) follow people, not brands. That lets a solo founder compete with companies 100x larger.
- It is a news-first platform. He cites a 2021 Pew study: 69% of US Twitter users get news there, so timely, opinionated posts about your industry travel well.
- Other benefits he lists: targeted traffic, lead generation, real-time feedback, public customer support, competitor monitoring, and free tools (ads are optional).

## Proof from his own account and other indie hackers

- **Ilias (@illyism):** started the year at 0 followers. He shared SEO tips, engaged with his audience, and reached 1,400+ followers. After Marc Louvion tweeted about his SEO services at MagicSpace, he got 20 solid leads in DMs almost overnight. He says Twitter became a top source of new business for his SEO agency, which went from $0 to $5,000 MRR in under a month. His takeaway: "It's important to connect with the right people."
- **Nick Buzz (UI design business):** went from struggling to $65k MRR in 3 months. He shared design process breakdowns instead of self-promotion, tweets regularly hit 100k+ impressions, and he took up to 10 calls a day from ideal prospects. Ilias's conclusion: building in public with tactical, value-driven content beat ads.
- **Marc Lou (ShipFast):** grew from 1k followers in July 2022 to 45k+ by openly sharing his journey. One ShipFast announcement tweet reached 167,000 impressions. He combined Twitter with Product Hunt, and Ilias's lesson is to build the audience first so the launch has social proof. Ilias cites Marc going from $1k MRR to $50k MRR in a year.

## Tools Ilias recommends

- **Typefully:** best for writing and posting across X and LinkedIn. It has an AI writing assistant, a distraction-free editor, scheduling for tweets and threads, and publishes to X, LinkedIn, Threads, Bluesky and Mastodon. The free plan is limited to 10 posts a month with no AI.
- **Tweet Hunter:** best for AI content and growth on X. It has a library of 12M+ viral tweets, scheduling and queues, and analytics. It is X only, with plans from $29/month (AI on the $49 plan).
- **StoryChief:** for teams running content across many channels (collaboration, multi-channel publishing, SEO tools).
- **PostEverywhere:** schedules one post to X and 11 other networks from one calendar. It has bulk CSV scheduling, a REST API and a hosted MCP server, from $9/month with a 7-day trial.
- **Hypefury:** dropped X support in 2026, and its X analytics tool BlackMagic shut down on July 1, 2026. If you post on X, he says to use Typefully or Tweet Hunter.
- **MagicMeme:** for making meme content quickly.

## Building the audience

- **Find your niche:** research hashtags like #buildinpublic, follow experts, micro-influencers, competitors and engaged users, and join those conversations.
- **Post value, not pitches:** insights, behind-the-scenes details, tips and actionable advice. Vary formats (threads, polls, quotes, infographics, memes), post regularly, and ask questions to get feedback.
- **Optimize the profile:** an informative bio that states your expertise and products, a link, links to your projects, a pinned tweet (a launch or best work), and a location set.
- **Engage daily:**
  - Reply to questions and comments, which improves the visibility of your tweets.
  - Like and retweet people in your community.
  - Follow back people who engage.
  - DM new followers to thank them.
- **Work with micro-influencers:** reply to and share their content (tagging them), and DM them discounts or free product in exchange for reviews.
- **Be patient:** "Consistency and persistence are key." You won't build a targeted audience overnight.

## Engagement tactics that turn followers into leads

- **Spaces and AMAs:** set a date and promote it beforehand, and tag influencers and invite them to join. Provide value during the chat without heavy self-promotion. Afterwards, recap the highlights in a thread and turn the insights into blog posts.
- **Contests and giveaways:** give away products, discounts or premium access, and require a follow and a retweet to enter. Run joint contests with influencers to expand reach.
- **Polls and surveys:** ask questions about your niche or product, use images or video, and track responses in a spreadsheet for new product, content or feature ideas. Follow up with the results.
- **Exclusive value:** coupon codes, early access, sneak peeks, and Twitter-only reviews, how-tos and case studies.
- **Highlight user wins:** ask users how your product helped, share the best stories as threads or testimonials, and tag the users so they share them with their own followers.

## Promoting your product without hard selling

The key is to "avoid overly promotional content," because followers quickly tune out hard sells. Weave product mentions in among the value you provide:

- Share product updates and explain which problem each new feature solves. Use images and video.
- Re-share the best reviews and tag the reviewers. Turn compelling stories into threads.
- Set up search alerts for your product name and handle, and respond to all feedback. Give support publicly so others see your responsiveness, and move to private if needed.
- Show use cases with real customers, step-by-step walkthroughs, and product mentions inside industry-analysis threads where the product solves the issue being discussed.

## Repurposing and cross-promotion

- **Threads to blog posts:** expand your best threads into long-form guides, with images and examples, and link back to the original thread.
- **LinkedIn:** repost threads in relevant groups, highlight them on your profile, and ask connections to share them.
- **Product Hunt:** share your PH post at the same time as the Twitter announcement, ask followers to upvote, and thank those who did afterward.
- **Newsletter:** feature excerpts of top threads, turn threads into infographics, and promote your handle in every email.

## Analytics and his 11-step blueprint

Track and refine based on: impressions, engagement rate (likes, retweets, replies), link clicks, top content, media performance (images, video and GIFs vs text), follower growth, demographics, and peak posting times.

His blueprint:
1. Set clear goals for followers, engagement, clicks and conversions.
2. Research your niche.
3. Grow the audience by consistently providing value.
4. Promote strategically, never overtly.
5. Monitor notifications and reply fast.
6. Engage followers with chats, contests, polls and perks.
7. Repurpose content.
8. Analyze the data.
9. Automate repetitive work with tools.
10. Be patient.
11. Plan a content calendar in Typefully or StoryChief and schedule ahead.
