## ChatGPT will not replace SEO, but it is rewriting it

- Ilias's short answer to "Will ChatGPT kill SEO?" is no, but it "is rewriting the rules." His view comes from running SEO sprints for SaaS, building LinkDR, and launching AISEOTracker to track how AI tools actually mention brands.
- ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude and Gemini do two things at once: they answer questions directly, and they choose which sources to cite and summarize. He calls that second part "the new SEO battleground."
- He frames the shift as SEO evolving into "AI visibility optimization," not dying. His line: "If ChatGPT doesn't mention you, it doesn't matter if you're #1 on Google."
- ChatGPT is replacing some parts of the search journey, not all of it.

## The goal moves from ranking to being cited and retold

- The old goal was to rank #1 on Google for a keyword. The new goal is to be the brand AI chooses to talk about when users ask questions.
- Ilias calls this "narrative SEO." The new game has three parts:
  - Get cited by authoritative content.
  - Be summarized well by AI.
  - Shape the narrative AI creates about your niche.
- Old-school tactics like keyword stuffing and random backlinks "won't cut it." AI reads deeper and picks what feels trustworthy, useful, and common across sources.
- His reframe: stop asking "How do I rank on Google?" and ask "How do I get cited, summarized, and spoken about by AI?"
- He says this frontier is "wide open."

## How ChatGPT changes the funnel

- People now ask ChatGPT instead of Googling. His examples:
  - "Best tools for content repurposing"
  - "How to use AI for customer support"
  - "What's the best GPT-powered chatbot?"
- These used to be Google queries. Now they go to ChatGPT, so a brand will not show up unless it is part of the answer.
- His summary: SEO is no longer just about being found, it is about "being remembered and re-told."

## Ilias's four-part playbook for AI visibility

1. **Track your AI visibility.** He built AISEOTracker to scan ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews and show whether a brand gets mentioned.
2. **Earn citations from real content.** Not PBNs or spammy guest posts. Create assets worth quoting.
3. **Write with AI summarization in mind.** Use clear structure, entity names, and helpful formatting, because "AI needs signal."
4. **Control your narrative.** Define what you want AI to say about your brand, then seed that message across your content, blog, and partnerships.
