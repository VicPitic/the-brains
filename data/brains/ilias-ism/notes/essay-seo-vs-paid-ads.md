## The $1,500 test: SEO vs Google Ads vs Meta Ads on GenPPT

Ilias split $1,500 evenly ($500 each) across Google Ads, Meta Ads and SEO/content for GenPPT, an AI presentation generator, and tracked it for 12 months.
- **Google Ads:** targeted "ai presentation maker", "AI PPT generator" and related keywords. Result: 1 trial signup, about $500 per acquisition, zero traffic after stopping.
- **Meta Ads:** interest-based targeting (entrepreneurs, students, startup founders). Result: 2 purchases, about $250 per acquisition, roughly 2x return ("barely break-even"), zero traffic after stopping.
- **SEO/content:** 5-6 long-tail blog posts, 10-15 outreach backlinks, tool directory and listicle listings. Result after 12 months: 100,000+ visitors, 3%+ conversion rate, 5,000+ monthly organic visitors, 50x+ return on $500.
- His conclusion: "The math isn't even close. For sustainable SaaS growth, content wins."
- Paid total was $1,000 for 3 conversions (about $333 per conversion). He says the $500 of content is worth 5-figure value over the next 3 years.

## One post drove 70% of traffic: "Gen Alpha Slang"

- One shareable, fun article, "Gen Alpha Slang: The Complete Guide", drove 70% of GenPPT's traffic. The audience (students, young professionals making presentations) matched the product's users.
- Results from that single post: 35,000+ visits in 12 months, 3,500+ visits per month now, 80+ ranking keywords, top 10 for "gen alpha slang" (20,000+ monthly searches).
- It cost about $100 to write. Getting 35,000 visitors through Google Ads at their CPC would have needed $10,000+.
- Caveat he acknowledges: viral posts like this bring traffic but not high-intent buyers.

## Why SEO compounds and ads are rented attention

- Ads are linear: spend $500 for about 200 clicks a month, stop paying and traffic drops to zero. "You're renting attention."
- SEO compounds. His example trajectory for a post: month 1 about 100 visits, month 3 about 500, month 6 about 1,500, month 12 about 3,500+ per month.
- Site-wide traffic: about 500/day in Dec 2024, about 700/day in Jun 2025, about 2,000/day in Jan 2026.
- The $500 spent on content in early 2025 still produces traffic; the $1,000 on ads was gone when he stopped.
- Cost per visitor: about $0.003 for SEO vs $5+ for ads.
- Timeline: expect 4-6 months for meaningful traffic. Posts started ranking at 3 months and peaked around months 8-12.

## Backlinks as a multiplier

- Content attracts backlinks that lift every page on the site, which ads cannot buy.
- In the last month GenPPT earned 40+ quality backlinks from DR 40-80 sites, including:
  - Chatbase Blog (DR 80): "ChatGPT Enterprise Alternatives" listicle
  - Tolt Blog (DR 65): founder mentioned GenPPT in an income report
  - VoiceNotes Blog (DR 53): "Meeting Summary Tools" roundup
  - Global Cybersecurity Network (DR 40): "Best AI Presentation Makers 2026"
  - StoryChief: tool directory listing, about 1,000 visitors a month alone
- Each backlink sends referral traffic, builds domain authority and improves rankings across the site.

## When paid ads still make sense

Ilias sees paid as useful for:
- Product launches that need immediate visibility
- Validating demand with a quick test before investing in SEO
- Retargeting visitors who didn't convert
- Competitive keywords where SEO would take years

For most SaaS companies as a primary growth channel, he says SEO wins long-term. Start SEO first if you have 6+ months of runway; the best strategy combines both, using PPC data to pick keywords and then building SEO content around them.

## What he'd do differently and the SEO starter playbook

Regrets:
1. Start SEO earlier. They waited 6 months before investing seriously; starting sooner would have given about 2x the traffic.
2. Write more bottom-funnel content. Now adding "GenPPT vs Canva" (comparison), "How to make an AI presentation" (how-to) and "Best AI pitch deck generator" (listicle).
3. Build distribution first. "Content + distribution = unstoppable." X/Twitter posts drove 20,000+ visitors, and directories and listicles thousands more. "Don't just publish, promote."

Playbook:
1. Find one long-tail keyword with 1,000-10,000 monthly searches.
2. Write the best article on it (2,000+ words, actually useful).
3. Build 5-10 backlinks via guest posts, directories or outreach.
4. Wait 6 months.
5. Measure traffic value by comparing what the same visitors would cost in ads.

He also recommends indexing tools to get new pages crawled in minutes instead of weeks.
