## Competitor link-gap outreach as the fastest route to money-page rankings

- Ilias's core method: pull the exact pages that already link to 2 or more of your direct SERP competitors. Those editors have linked to tools in your category, so the pitch is "relevant by definition."
- Steps:
  1. Enter your top 3-5 ranking competitors into a backlink checker (LinkDR's free one, or Ahrefs).
  2. Filter to Dofollow, 1 link per domain, and exclude customer asset subdomains (`cdn.`, widgets, badges).
  3. Require at least 1,000 monthly organic visits on the linking domain. He cites 43% of DR 30+ domains getting under 1,000 visits a month, so filter on traffic, not just DR.
  4. Pitch the author or editor directly with a specific reason your page adds value to their article.
- Cost: $160-$500 managed, or $90-$250 in DIY publisher fees. First link in 2-4 weeks. Scales without headcount.
- He ranks this and listicle insertions as the fastest ranking lift for money pages.

## "Best X" listicle insertions to win both Google and AI search answers

- Ilias's logic: when a buyer asks ChatGPT or Google "What is the best [category] tool?", the answer engine reads the top-ranking "10 Best [Category] Tools" articles and synthesizes their picks.
- Getting added to 5-10 ranking listicles in your niche gives three wins at once:
  - Referral traffic from high-intent buyers comparing options.
  - A contextual dofollow backlink on a page already in Google's top 10.
  - Immediate inclusion in ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Overview answers.
- Method: identify every ranking roundup where competitors appear, then negotiate your inclusion.
- Cost $160-$400, first link in 1-3 weeks.
- He states that pages ranking in Google's top 10 are the number one source pool cited by ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews, so a link strategy should serve ranking and AI citation together.

## Niche edits and guest posts: pricing evidence and execution rules

- Niche edit (contextual insertion into an existing, indexed article): link equity transfers faster than waiting for a new post to index, because Google has already crawled and ranked the host page. Cost $160-$400, 1-3 weeks.
- From his analysis of 10,030 publisher quotes: 49% charge the exact same fee for a link insertion as for a guest post, and 38% charge less. Median publisher fee for a DR 30-59 guest post or insertion is $90-$100 upfront. 84% require upfront payment, and resellers mark up 1.44x. Median DR 30+ ask is $100.
- Rule for niche edits: never force an awkward exact-match anchor into an unrelated paragraph. Write a 1-2 sentence addition that improves the section so the editor keeps it permanently.
- Guest posts only work when written for a real publication's audience, not "generic AI filler to a link farm." What works in 2026:
  - Pitch an angle the blog hasn't covered (teardown, template, benchmark).
  - Include custom diagrams or real product screenshots.
  - Place the target link naturally, alongside 1-2 links to authoritative non-competing sources.
- Guest posts cost $160-$500 with a 3-4 week lead time. His outsourced offer runs from $160 per placed link.

## Passive link assets: original data pages and free micro-tools

- **Original data/statistics pages**: journalists, bloggers and AI writers constantly search "<topic> statistics 2026". Publish proprietary numbers from your own database or customers and writers cite and link for free.
  - His example: LinkDR's Link Building Statistics page built on 10,030 publisher quotes. Every stat has a clean copy-paste citation sentence so writers can reference it in seconds.
  - Cost: $0 publisher fee (research time). 4-8 weeks to first links, compounding to DR 60-90+ editorial citations.
- **Free interactive micro-tools**: a narrow, zero-signup calculator, checker or generator "earns far more organic links than another 2,000-word beginner's guide."
  - Examples: LinkDR's Competitor Backlink Checker and Domain Email Finder; ROI calculators, template generators or graders that solve one painful step of the buyer's workflow.
  - Cost: $0 publisher fee, 1-3 days of dev. 4-12 weeks to links, evergreen for homepage and tool links.
- He treats both as compounding over 6-12 months.

## Low-cost and free tactics: PR quotes, mentions, broken links, partners

- **Journalist quote platforms** (Featured, Qwoted, Source of Sources, Connectively-style feeds): among the safest ways to get DR 70-90 homepage links. $0-$300 a month, 2-6 weeks, low scalability because it takes founder time.
- **Unlinked brand mention reclamation**: when a blog, podcast show note or newsletter names your brand or founder without a link, send a friendly 3-sentence email thanking the author and asking them to make it clickable. Conversion "routinely beat[s] 25%" because the writer already vouched for you. Free, 1-2 weeks, capped by the number of mentions.
- **Broken link building**: in Ahrefs "Best by links" filtered to 404 on competitor or niche resource pages, find a dead guide with 20+ referring domains. Publish an updated replacement and email the sites linking to the dead URL. $0-$100, 3-6 weeks.
- **Integration, partner and customer links**: apply to the official integration directories of tools you connect to (Slack, Stripe, HubSpot, Notion, Zapier, MCP registries) and co-author a setup guide. These carry high topical trust and send qualified referral buyers. Free, 2-4 weeks, highly scalable for B2B SaaS and developer platforms.

## The 90-day link building plan

- **Month 1 (foundation and quick wins)**: submit to 15-20 curated directories, ship 1 citeable data post or free tool, and order 3-5 DR 30-40+ contextual backlinks to the homepage and primary service page.
- **Month 2 (competitor and listicle gap)**: run competitor backlink outreach to get added to the top 5-10 "Best [Category]" roundups ranking in Google's top 10.
- **Month 3 (scale and monitor)**: point monthly DR 40-60+ links at the highest-converting page-2 articles to push them into the top 3, and monitor every live backlink for uptime and dofollow status.
- Ilias's framing of the trade-off: doing outreach in-house costs about 35 hours of cold outreach per campaign. His service sells placements from $160 per link (15% off on monthly plans), most live in 3-4 weeks, paying only for verified live placements.
