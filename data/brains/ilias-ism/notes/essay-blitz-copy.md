## Blitz Copy is built on emotion, not explanation

- Ilias created "Blitz Copy" as a copywriting method for landing pages, emails, LinkedIn posts, cold emails, tweets, YouTube intros, TikToks and SEO titles.
- His core claim: "People don't buy what they understand. They buy what excites them."
- He argues that explanation doesn't sell, and that emotion does. Copy should interrupt, ignite and provoke within seconds, because people face infinite scroll and endless options.
- The target reaction is: "Wait. That's me. I need this. Why haven't I seen this before?"
- The goal is a dopamine-driven "YES! Aha! Wow! I need this!" feeling that drives action right now (click, visit, share, buy).
- He describes it as "chemical warfare for conversions" and wants it to feel almost "illegal" in its effectiveness.

## The four parts of a Blitz headline and the four-step sequence

Blitz Copy uses four ingredients: dopamine triggers (tension, curiosity, instant relief), identity spikes (readers feel "that's me"), pattern interrupts (stop the scroll cold) and emotional payoffs (drive action immediately). It follows a fixed sequence:

1. **Identity Trigger.** Make them feel instantly recognized. Example: "You're not disorganized. You're drowning in 42 browser tabs and guilt."
2. **Emotional Hook.** Surface a hidden frustration or desire. Example: "You've tried every productivity app. None of them stuck past day 3."
3. **Mini Cliffhanger.** Build tension to keep them reading. Example: "We tested this on 1,324 people. Here's what happened next..."
4. **Specific Outcome (the payoff).** Promise a concrete, desirable result. Example: "Reclaim 2 hours a day, without changing your habits."

## The neurochemical triggers

Ilias lists the hidden levers that move people from "maybe" to "I need this now":
- **Curiosity:** "What am I missing?"
- **FOMO:** "Others are getting ahead, am I?"
- **Surprise:** "Wait, I didn't expect that!"
- **Relief:** "Finally, a solution that just works."
- **Urgency:** "If I don't act now, I'll lose out."
- **Validation:** "This is for people like me."
- **Desire to belong:** "I want to be part of this."
- **Power / advantage:** "I finally found an edge no one else knows."
- **Unique secret:** access to something exclusive or hidden.
- **Domination / exclusivity:** being among the first, or the only ones, to benefit.

## Know your target cold (the "empathy engine")

- Blitz Copy only works if the writer understands the audience deeply: not just who they are, but what keeps them up at night and what they secretly want.
- Generic hype doesn't work. The copy should reflect the audience's real pains and desires in their own words and worldview, so it feels personal and urgent.
- Ilias turned this into an installable agent skill called `understand-customers`, which works in Cursor, Claude Code or any AI (`npx skills add Illyism/seo-skills --skill understand-customers`).
- The prompt he uses has four research blocks:
  - **Core identity and role:** who they really are (e.g. "ambitious SaaS founder", "overwhelmed marketing manager", "freelancer struggling to scale") and their primary goal related to the problem.
  - **Deepest pains (the "nightmare scenario"):** their most visceral nightmare, the exact words they use, their inner monologue, what they tried that failed and why, and the emotional consequences (feeling stuck, wasting money, falling behind competitors, imposter syndrome).
  - **Unspoken desires (the "magic wand" outcome):** the almost "too good to be true" outcome, what it means emotionally or for status (market leader, peace of mind, industry recognition, freedom), and what would feel like a "cheat code" or "unfair advantage".
  - **Language and worldview:** their jargon, tools and influencers, and a core belief about the problem that the product can affirm or satisfyingly challenge.
- Output of the prompt: initial Blitz Copy angles (identity triggers, emotional hooks, pattern interrupts, dopamine-driven payoffs).

## Before and after: GenPPT headline rewrite

- **Before:** "AI Presentation Generator. Make presentations with AI. Save time. No experience required."
- **After:** "Still Wasting Hours on Slides? Let AI Build Your Deck in Seconds. Stop fighting templates and design blocks. GenPPT turns your ideas into viral-ready presentations, automatically. No design skills, no wasted time, just instant wow."
- The pattern: replace a feature label with a pain-first question (identity trigger plus emotional hook), then a concrete speed outcome.

## Before and after: LinkDR and AISEOTracker

**LinkDR**
- **Before:** "AI Link Building Tool. Find backlinks for your website."
- **After:** "Still Paying $5K/Month for Shady Backlinks? LinkDR automates high-quality placements, for $100 each. No agencies. No retainers."
- The rewrite leads with a specific painful number ($5K/month), then contrasts it with a concrete price ($100 per placement) and removes the objections (agencies, retainers).

**AISEOTracker**
- **Before:** "Get AI to recommend your brand, not your competitors. Stop losing sales to AI recommendations. See exactly which brands AI mentions when users search your industry, and get actionable data to improve your position."
- **After:** "Invisible in AI Search? See Who's Winning, and Steal Their Rankings. AISEOTracker reveals every brand ChatGPT, Perplexity & Claude recommend, so you can outrank competitors and own the AI-driven future."
- The rewrite uses a pain question, names the specific AI products, and adds a "steal their rankings" advantage angle.

## Blitz Copy as the engine behind a campaign

- Ilias treats Blitz Copy as "the emotional engine behind every Blitz campaign": it turns attention into action and powers the rush that makes people buy, share and join.
- He wants the final jolt ("I'm not 100% sure, but I need this") to be engineered, not accidental. It's the same dopamine-driven spark behind his Blitz SEO approach (a focused 30-day sprint to rank one high-value page for one money keyword).
- His stance: "We don't write to impress. We write to convert. To provoke. To dominate attention."
- His summary: "Be first. Be loud. Be unignorable."
