## Hampton: an executive peer group for CEOs, built from a peer group Sam ran at The Hustle

Sam announced a new company called Hampton (joinhampton.com), described as "an executive peer group for CEOs." He expects it "will be amazing."

- Origin: he started it because of a peer group he created while running The Hustle. He says that group "changed my life."
- What the peer group did for him: it helped him sell The Hustle and helped him grow it, "everything."
- The format he chose to scale: CEOs meeting in a peer group, the same mechanism that worked for him personally rather than a new, untested model.

## Hampton membership criteria: $1M minimum revenue, ~$20M average

Sam states who Hampton is built for.

- Entry bar: members need at least $1M in revenue.
- Typical member: the average member has about $20M in revenue.
- Target "sweet spot": CEOs whose companies fall in that range. His pitch to them is simply "COME."
- The audience is CEOs specifically, not founders at large, so the peer group is composed of people running real businesses with revenue.

## Why Sam builds from his own experience: product-from-personal-use signal

The essay gives a short, concrete example of how Sam picks what to build.

- He built Hampton because he personally relied on a peer group while running The Hustle and credited it with outcomes that mattered most: growing the company and selling it.
- The reasoning is firsthand: he used the thing, saw it change his results, and then built a company around making it available to others.
- The recommendation he makes to prospects is grounded in outcomes he attributes to the peer group (a sale and growth), not in abstract benefits.

## How Sam launched and promoted Hampton: a direct call-to-action to his audience

The post shows his announcement approach.

- Channel: a short note on his own blog (originally on his old blog, The Anti-MBA), reposted to samparr.com on March 20, 2023.
- Structure: one line naming the company, a one-sentence definition, a personal origin story, the membership qualifier, and a link. No long pitch.
- Qualification built into the pitch: stating the revenue floor ($1M) and the average ($20M) up front lets the right CEOs self-select and signals the quality of the group.
- Call to action: a direct link to joinhampton.com, with a plain invitation: "if you're a CEO and wanna join…check it."
