## Natural moneymakers: who they are and how they differ from other rich founders

Sam uses "natural moneymakers" for entrepreneurs who are "so violently good at making money it looks like cheating." He says he noticed them across the 850 podcast episodes he has done, and that almost no one talks about the pattern. He has interviewed about 10 of them and met a few more inside Hampton, his founder community.

## Where natural moneymakers show up and what they've earned

- They tend to work in roles built on deal making, brokering and inserting themselves into a situation.
- They rarely build in public.
- They have made between $100m and $1b, in different fields.
- The trait is not tied to one industry. It shows up across fields.

## Being rich is not the same as being a natural moneymaker

- Sam says you can be successful and rich and still not be a natural moneymaker.
- His example is Ted Turner, who founded CNN and became a multi-billionaire. Sam says he was not a natural moneymaker.
- Turner's strengths, per Sam: he "had vision, took risks, and was charismatic."
- Natural moneymakers are a different type. The edge is not vision, risk appetite or charisma.

## They are unemotional about business

- Sam's first defining attribute: "They are unemotional about business."
- Natural moneymakers feel anxiety like everyone else. The difference is how they use emotion.
- Sam contrasts this with the usual founder story: being emotional and passionate helps founders succeed.
- Natural moneymakers are "more cold about money. More logical."
- Practical reading: they treat money decisions as logic problems and keep feelings from steering the deal.

## Context for the claim

- The essay is the opening piece of Sam's newsletter, written to introduce it. He says his job is talking to billionaires, heads of massive companies and the best in their field, and the letter passes on what they tell him.
- Sam defines a rich life as a big career, being fit, and a family who loves him.
- The full list of attributes sits behind a subscriber wall, so only the traits above are covered here.
